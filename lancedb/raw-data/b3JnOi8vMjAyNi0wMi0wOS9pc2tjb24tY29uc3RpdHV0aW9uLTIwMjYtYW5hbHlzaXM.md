# ISKCON 2026 Constitution: Comprehensive Analysis of Deviations, Deficiencies, and Crisis of Institutional Legitimacy

## Summary
Analysis comparing ISKCON's 2026 Constitution against modern constitutional standards and Prabhupada's recorded instructions. The document fails as both a constitution (no separation of powers, no bill of rights, no judicial review) and as a spiritual charter (contradicts founder's instructions on decentralization, book preservation, elections, austerity, and member participation).

## Key Findings
- GBC holds all three branches of power with no independent judiciary
- No enforceable individual rights for devotees
- 1970 Direction of Management electoral mandate completely ignored
- No textual protection for Prabhupada's original books
- BBT 50/50 fund division not mandated
- No austerity standards for sannyasis
- Varnashrama (50% of the mission) absent
- Psychological control: administrative authority fused with spiritual authority to silence dissent
- Bengaluru case study shows internal governance failures escalate to decades-long secular lawsuits
- Power hierarchy: rules heaviest on ordinary devotees, lightest on GBC members

## Ten Deviations
1. Guru system: bureaucratic approval vs spiritual qualification
2. GBC: centralized power vs traveling preachers
3. Elections: ignored Direction of Management
4. Books: no textual protection
5. BBT funds: 50/50 rule not mandated
6. Sannyasis: no austerity standards
7. Varnashrama: absent
8. Child protection: reactive not preventive
9. Women diksha gurus: calculated ambiguity
10. Downward accountability: nonexistent
