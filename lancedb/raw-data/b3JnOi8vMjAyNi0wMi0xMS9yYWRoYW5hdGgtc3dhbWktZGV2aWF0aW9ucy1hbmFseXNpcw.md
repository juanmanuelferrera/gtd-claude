# Radhanath Swami: Analysis of Deviations

Comprehensive analysis of Radhanath Swami's documented deviations from Gaudiya Vaishnava standards. Topics: radhanath swami, iskcon, new vrindaban, sulochan, kirtanananda, mayavadi, deviations, child abuse, prabhupada.

## 1. Murder Conspiracy: Sulochan Das (1986)
Multiple witnesses (Janmastami dasa, Kuladri dasa, Dharmatma dasa, Jyotirdhama dasa) implicate Radhanath in ordering the murder of Sulochan das. Documented in Henry Doktorski's "Killing for Krishna" (2018). Radhanath relocated to India in 1986 precisely when the murder occurred. Subpoenaed before Federal Grand Jury 1989, never charged. GBC reinstated him in 1994 with condition that US Government does not indict him.

## 2. New Vrindaban: 15 Years in Criminal Commune (1972-1986)
Lived at New Vrindaban during mail fraud, racketeering, murder, sexual abuse, child abuse, and drug operations. Received sannyasa from Kirtanananda. Maintained loyalty until at least 1990. Only distanced publicly in 1993.

## 3. Mayavadi / Impersonalist Influence
"The Journey Home" glorifies impersonalist teachers Prabhupada condemned. 53-essay critique by Kaliya Mardana dasa. Glorifies Swami Muktananda (exposed serial predator). Presents multiple paths as equally valid, contradicting Prabhupada's exclusive bhakti emphasis.

## 4. Deviations from Prabhupada's Instructions
Opened hospital (Prabhupada rejected), introduced yoga classes (Prabhupada rejected), multi-path approach, independent ashrams, minimizes Prabhupada on website, claims ISKCON projects as personal, builds personal power structure through sannyasa disciples.

## 5. Protection of Child Abusers
Dhanurdhara Swami found guilty of child abuse by ISKCON Child Protection Office but is regular speaker at Radhanath's Bhakti Center in New York. Radhanath reportedly opposed excommunication of a child abuser.

## 6. Celebrity-Guru Persona
Website brands as "Teacher. Author. Activist." Modern personal brand rather than traditional Vaishnava presentation. Bhakti Center operates as wellness hub. Interfaith associations with Dalai Lama and others contradicting Prabhupada's instructions.
